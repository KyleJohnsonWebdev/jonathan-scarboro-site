How the consultation works

  1. You reach out. Email me or send a message through Psychology Today. A line or two is plenty.
  2. We find a time. I typically respond within one to two business days.
  3. We talk for 20–30 minutes. Free, so we can get a feel for each other and see if working together makes sense.
